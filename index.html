Operations & Policies



01. Development Standards

All projects follow strict modular programming guidelines. We ensure every line of code is documented, clean, and optimized for speed. We do not use "spaghetti" logic; everything is built for long-term maintenance.

02. Turnaround & Delivery

Standard business websites are delivered within 14-21 business days. Complex enterprise solutions are scoped individually with clear milestones and weekly progress reports.

03. Support & Maintenance

We provide 3 months of technical support after project delivery to ensure stability. Ongoing maintenance packages are available for security patches and performance monitoring.
