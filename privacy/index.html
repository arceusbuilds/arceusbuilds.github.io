Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2, 2026

Arceusbuilds ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at arceusbuilds.github.io or engage our professional software and cloud hosting services.

1. Comprehensive Information Collection

To deliver our highly specialized software environments, custom builds, and maintenance routines, we collect data falling into the following distinct categories:

  • Directly Provided Personal Data: When you inquire about our services, sign up for a meta-platform account, or enter a business agreement, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and physical billing/business address.
  • Operational & Project Data: This includes the source code, custom application logic, database structures, API keys, and configuration files required to execute your custom software build or SaaS platform.
  • Automatically Collected Technical Data: When navigating our website or utilizing our cloud-deployed interfaces, our servers automatically log your IP address, browser type, operating system, device identification numbers, and granular usage patterns (timestamps, pages viewed, and requested URLs).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We strictly process your data only for legitimate business purposes mandated by our operational agreements. This includes:

  • Fulfilling our contract to architect, personalize, and deploy your custom web applications.
  • Managing your secure cloud instances on third-party networks such as Amazon Web Services (AWS).
  • Executing routine system maintenance, push security patches, and debugging technical errors.
  • Ensuring compliance with local laws, auditing requirements, and preventing malicious cybersecurity attacks against our meta-platforms.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Multi-Tenant Data Storage

AWS Ecosystem: For clients electing to utilize our hosting services, any data flowing through your application is processed and stored via top-tier third-party cloud infrastructure, heavily relying on Amazon Web Services (AWS).

Security Responsibility Model: Arceusbuilds manages the software-level security (e.g., encryption of files, secure routing, and database firewalls). However, the physical server architecture, network hardware, and foundational database uptime are managed exclusively by AWS.

Third-Party Liability: In the highly unlikely event of a massive, global AWS data breach or catastrophic server failure leading to data leaks, Arceusbuilds cannot be held legally liable for the compromise of your data, provided we executed standard industry security configurations on our end.

4. Data Retention & Destruction Policy

We do not store your personal or operational data indefinitely. We retain your data only for as long as is strictly necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with professional auditing laws.

Upon the formal termination of a maintenance agreement or a specific project contract, and subject to full settlement of due fees, Arceusbuilds will facilitate the transfer of your primary database assets over to you. Following a standard 30-day grace period post-termination, any redundant backups or active instances hosted on our localized cloud profiles will be securely wiped and permanently deleted.

5. Zero Data-Selling Guarantee

Arceusbuilds does not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal or business data to third-party marketing companies under any circumstances. We only disclose your information to:

  • Third-party cloud infrastructure vendors (such as AWS) directly required to host and execute your application.
  • Legal and government authorities if required to do so by law, court order, or to protect the vital security of our network.

6. Your Data Rights

Operating primarily out of Nagpur, Maharashtra, we respect your rights regarding the processing of your data under applicable Indian digital data protection frameworks. You maintain the right to:

  • Request access to the exact personal and contact data we hold about your profile.
  • Demand the correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time (which may lead to the termination of active cloud hosting or maintenance routines if that data is strictly required to operate the application).

7. Policy Modifications

Arceusbuilds reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy at any time to account for shifting global data laws or changes to our AWS cloud operations. Any modifications will be declared on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. Continued use of our site or services after a policy shift constitutes active acceptance of the updated terms.
